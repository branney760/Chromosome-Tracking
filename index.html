Your lab bench

Use taps to track chromosomes in fictional California exemplarius, the California teaching bloom (2n = 4; n = 2). No printing, dragging, spreadsheet, screenshots or purchased materials are required.

Complete the cell-division tutorial first. Plan about 60–90 minutes for modeling, repeating comparisons and writing explanations; take breaks as needed. The activity is untimed.

How the chromosome labels work

Long A and short B identify two homologous pairs. M and P identify parental origin, also shown in blue and orange. One X is a replicated chromosome: two sisters connected at the black centromere. After sisters separate, each rod is one chromosome. Labels 1 and 2 identify descendants of sisters so you can track them; they do not indicate different homologs.

All drawings are position models, not microscope images. The exchange activity shows only the A pair; the B pair remains unchanged.
